Nº I

Field marks

How to identify the species
  • —First in the queue when a waitlist opens.
  • —Posts first impressions before the reviews land.
  • —India angle, always.
  • —Sceptical of hype; feeds on launch days.

Field observers note a consistent pattern. The specimen surfaces wherever access is scarcest — closed betas, staggered rollouts, midnight launches — tests what it finds properly rather than skimming the announcement, and files its report in public, supplying the India angle that is so often missing from everyone else’s. It is, by every account, an early bird; the waitlists know its name.
